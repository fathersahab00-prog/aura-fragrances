Inquiries & Assistance

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Yes, we provide expedited Pan-India delivery across all serviceable postal codes in India. Every fragrance flacon is cradled in our custom impact-resistant luxury presentation box with tamper-proof security seals to ensure immaculate transit.
Our inaugural collection features 8 signature compositions: Obsidian Aura & Monarch Noir (For Him), Velvet Aurelia & Golden Sillage (For Her), Aura Sublime & Celestial Santal (Unisex), and our private reserve editions Imperial Aura & Elysian Mist (Best Sellers). Available in 50ml and 100ml flacons.
You can place an order directly on our website through our integrated shopping bag and checkout system, or enjoy personalized concierge shopping by placing your order directly through WhatsApp with one tap.
Yes, Cash on Delivery (COD) is available on orders across major serviceable cities in India with a quick phone or WhatsApp verification prior to dispatch.
We accept all primary digital payment modes including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex), Net Banking, and direct WhatsApp payment transfers.
Orders placed before 2:00 PM are dispatched on the same business day. Delivery timelines are typically 2–3 business days for tier-1 metropolitan cities and 4–6 business days for the rest of India via express air courier.
Due to the hygiene nature of personal luxury fragrances, opened flacons cannot be returned. However, if your order arrives damaged or defective in transit, we provide a 7-day complimentary door-to-door replacement guarantee. Simply reach out to our concierge with an unboxing photo or video.
